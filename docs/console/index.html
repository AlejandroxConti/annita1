The 85 points in this replay are a real sample drawn from the full 415-frame run (stability validation stage, ~19.6 minutes, ESP32-S3 camera alternating between land and water scenes). This is not synthetic data: sharpness, contrast, color, TinyML confidence and the SVS decision for every frame shown come directly from the log captured on the RT1170's M7 core. The totals on the right panel correspond to the complete 415-frame run.

The small reference thumbnail is a real Sentinel-2 satellite image from the EuroSAT dataset used to train the on-device TinyML model — it is not what the onboard ESP32-S3 camera captured. The camera photographs physical land/water samples as a stand-in sensor; Annita-1's contribution is the processing pipeline itself, which is sensor-agnostic and was trained on real satellite imagery.
